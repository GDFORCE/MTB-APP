How it works

From a protocol PDF to a patient's calendar

Five steps, one continuous record. Scroll to follow a trial through the app.

01

Upload the protocol

Add a trial and upload its protocol document. The trial details are read from it and shown for you to check before anything is saved.

  • Protocol ID, CTRI number, phase, indication and drug filled in
  • Sample size, duration and total visits captured
  • You review every field before the trial is saved
Done by
Sponsor, CRO, SMO or site team
You get
A trial with reviewed details

10 fields extracted

02

AI drafts the visit schedule

The schedule of assessments is rebuilt as a structured template that you can read, check and edit on your phone.

  • Visit number, name and type
  • Gap between visits, positive and negative windows
  • Activities due at each visit

“This is an AI-generated schedule and may contain mistakes. Please check carefully before saving.”

Done by
The app, from your protocol
You get
A draft visit schedule template

AI draft · 2 items to review

03

You review and approve it

Nothing is approved automatically. Items that need a human check are marked, and the template is yours to correct.

  • Edit visits, gaps, windows and activities
  • Add comments for your team
  • Every save creates a version; earlier versions are never destroyed
Done by
You and your team
You get
An approved, versioned template

Template v3 saved

04

An anchor date becomes a schedule

Add a patient with a Subject ID and initials, enter the anchor date and generate. Each visit gets its date and window from the approved template.

  • Screening or randomization date as the anchor
  • Visit dates and window periods calculated for you
  • Add one patient, or upload many at once
Done by
PI or research team
You get
A dated calendar for the patient

12 visits generated

05

Visits happen on time

The site records the actual visit date and status. The next visit moves up, and the right people hear about it.

  • Reminders 1 week before the window opens, 1 day before and on the day
  • Overdue visits alert the team daily for three days, then weekly
  • Recruitment counters update with every status change
Done by
PI or research team
You get
Updated status, counters and reminders

Role-based access

One trial. Each role sees its own view.

Every screen shows only what the signed-in person is permitted to see. Pick a role to see its app.

Recruitment across every site, with no patient identity

Create trials, review the schedule, add sites and follow enrolment as it happens.

Sees

  • Trials, sites and principal investigators
  • Screened, randomized, withdrawn and completed counts, per trial and per site
  • Schedule template, version history and protocol deviations

Never sees

Sees

  • Today's, upcoming and overdue visits, with the site name

Limited until granted

  • Without it: basic trial details, the schedule, Subject ID and initials only

Today's visits, by subject

Investigators and coordinators add patients, generate schedules and record what happened at each visit.

Sees and does

  • Patients on their authorized trials, by Subject ID and initials
  • Records the actual visit date and status
  • Gives a reason for screen failure, withdrawal or dropout

Never sees

  • Patients on trials or sites they are not assigned to

The next visit, and what to expect

Patients join from an invitation, verify their phone number and land on their own trial.

Sees

  • Their site and investigator
  • Reminders and messages from the research team

View only

  • Visit status is updated by the research team, not the patient
  • No access to any other patient's information

Runs the service without reading the trial

The platform

Everything that happens around a visit

Scheduling is the core. These are the tools that keep sponsors, sites and patients moving together.

Day, week and month views. Research teams see every subject's visit across their trials; patients see only their own. Scheduled, completed and overdue visits are told apart at a glance.

Compliance

Built for regulated work

Every significant action leaves a time-stamped record that no one can edit or delete, including the platform's own administrators.

  • Audit trail

    Who, when, which role and organization, the previous value, the new value and the reason for critical changes.

  • Least-privilege access

    Access follows role and assignment. Confidential protocols stay with authorized trial users, and chat stays with its participants.

  • Account security

    Phone and email verification by one-time code, security questions, strong password rules, lockout after repeated failed sign-ins and session expiry.

Designed to align with ICH-GCP, NDCT Rules 2019, Indian data privacy requirements, HIPAA, GDPR and 21 CFR Part 11, as applicable to your deployment.
