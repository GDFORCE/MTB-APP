About Trial page — requirements and role visibility

As of 2026-10-06

The About Trial page shows one trial only to people who have a relationship with it. Patients see plain-language study information and their own schedule; site staff and sponsors see operational data with subjects de-identified; platform admins see aggregates only. The server must send each role only its own fields. Hiding a field in the app is not enough.

Page content

Patient view: six tabs in plain language: Overview, Visit schedule, Medication, Risks and side effects, Contacts, FAQ.

Staff and sponsor view: one shared screen with header, recruitment funnel, sites, trial team, patients, visit schedule and documents.

  • A value nobody entered shows "Not provided". The page never fills it with a default or an estimate.
  • If one part fails to load, the rest still shows, with a notice and a retry button.
  • A patient with no enrolment sees "No enrolled study found", never another trial.

Access rules

  • Who can open it. Patient: enrolled in the trial through their own account. PI: created the trial, belongs to its organisation, is assigned to one of its subjects, or accepted an invitation. CRC: the same, without the organisation route. Sponsor and CRO: the trial is owned by or granted to their organisation. Platform admin: every trial, through the admin console only.
  • Everyone else is refused, and the trial does not appear in their lists.
  • Patient privacy. Nobody sees a patient's name, phone, email or date of birth on this page. Subjects appear as subject ID and initials.
  • Actions. Edit trial details: the owning sponsor or CRO, or the PI who created the trial. Add patient: PI and CRC. Add site: sponsor and CRO. Upload document: sponsor, CRO, PI and CRC. Patients and platform admins have no edit actions.

Not met today

  • The trial list the patient app calls (GET /trials) returns the full trial record, including enrolment count, target, creator and site names. The patient screen does not show them, but the server sends them.
  • The contact lookup behind the Contacts tab (GET /messaging/recipients) returns full user records for the care team, not only name, role, phone and email.
  • SMO and Site accounts pass the trial access check but are refused by the recruitment, subjects, team, documents and versions calls, so the staff view cannot load for them. Found by reading the code, not reproduced in the running app.
  • When duration is not entered, the patient view shows a figure worked out from the first and last visit dates, without saying it is an estimate.
  • The patient screen is English only. Hindi translations exist in the app, but this screen does not use them.
