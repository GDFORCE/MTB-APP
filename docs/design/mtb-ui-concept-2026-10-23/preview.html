Design concept · Fictional data
Proposed desktop navigation and reporting

Desktop is a proposed layout extension. Search shortcuts, filters, charts, and navigation are illustrative and require a separate implementation decision.

A visual language with room to breathe.

Quiet surfaces. Deliberate emphasis. Information you can trust.

05 / DESIGN LANGUAGE
01 · Palette

Clinical, with a human side.

Warm neutral surfaces, forest ink, and a purposeful teal. Amber signals review; red is reserved for overdue or blocked work.

02 · Typography

Read first. Decorate second.

Figtree keeps continuity with the app. Larger headings, legible body text, and short metadata lines establish a dependable hierarchy.

03 · Structure

One clear next step.

Priorities before totals for clinical users. The next appointment before progress for patients. Consistent cards and calmer navigation.

04 · Honest states

Uncertainty stays visible.

Missing anchors remain unresolved. Unknown times stay “Time not set.” Empty, loading, error, and permission states need equally careful treatment.
