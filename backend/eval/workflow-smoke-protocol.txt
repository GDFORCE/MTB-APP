SYNTHETIC SOFTWARE TEST PROTOCOL WF-001. Not a clinical treatment protocol.
Title: Schedule workflow test. Phase 1. Indication: software validation.

readability. Both tables apply to all the same enrolled participants; there
are no separately assigned substudies, cohorts or treatment arms.
Day 1 is the date of first dose (FIRST_DOSE). Clinical days have no Day 0.
The site records the actual LAST_DOSE date separately.
